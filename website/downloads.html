Installing

Setup takes about a minute either way. Whichever you choose, finish by signing in from the Account tab — it backs up your tasks and keeps your phone and browser in step.

Android (APK)

  1. Download the APK on your phone.
  2. Open it. If asked, allow Install unknown apps for your browser or Files app.
  3. Tap Install, open DYDTT, allow notifications for reminders, then sign in from the Account tab.

This is a beta build, so Android may show a Play Protect warning. Check the SHA-256 above matches your file.

Web app

  1. Open the web app in your browser.
  2. Use the browser's Install / Add to Home Screen option.
  3. Open the Account tab and sign in so your tasks match your phone.

Browsers can't do the Android-only secret game or background reminders; everything else works.

Updating

Download the newer APK and install it over the existing app. Sign in beforehand so everything is safely backed up, and your tasks will be waiting once the update is done. The web app updates itself.

Verify your download

Compare the SHA-256 above with your file's checksum. On Windows run certutil -hashfile DYDTT-v0.5b.apk SHA256; on macOS or Linux run shasum -a 256 DYDTT-v0.5b.apk. The two values should match exactly.
